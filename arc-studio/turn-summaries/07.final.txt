Here's what changed:

**1. Chain-aware contract addresses**

`CHAIN_DEFAULTS` in `App.tsx` maps chain ID → canonical address. The effective address is `contractOverride ?? CHAIN_DEFAULTS[chainId]`. When the user switches chains and was showing a known default for the old chain, it clears automatically. The "Testnet default" / "Mainnet default" reset button in `ContractBar` always targets whichever chain is active.

**URL params:** `?net=arcMainnet` or `?net=arcTestnet` triggers a `switchChain` on mount. `?contract=0x…` pre-fills the address override. Both params stack: `?net=arcMainnet&contract=0xabc…` selects mainnet and pins that address.

**2. Mainnet warning badge**

When `chainId === 5042`: a full-width amber banner above the header reads "MAINNET — real USDC — Transactions on this network use real funds and are irreversible." A smaller pulsing "Mainnet" badge also appears inline in the app title.

**3. Dynamic gas pricing (`src/hooks/useDynamicFees.ts`)**

Polls `eth_getBlockByNumber("latest")` every 4 seconds and computes:
- `maxFeePerGas = max(2 × baseFeePerGas, 20 gwei) + 1 gwei`
- `maxPriorityFeePerGas = 1 gwei`

Falls back to `{21 gwei, 1 gwei}` until the first RPC reply arrives or on transient errors. All four write calls — `approve`, `createEscrow`, `release`, `refund` — now use these live values instead of the hardcoded 20 gwei constant.
